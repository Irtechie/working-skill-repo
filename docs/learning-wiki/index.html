when considering multiple concurrent agent or model attempts for one workflow

optimize-cost-per-valid-result

when comparing direct, routed, speculative, or multi-agent execution strategies

compare total input, output, retry, correction, fan-in, and failed-attempt cost per deterministically valid result; do not infer efficiency from output tokens, billed model price, or nominal parallel speed alone

  • Curated ATV Community Call and Hack & Furious evidence (2026-07-09 through 2026-07-21): cost discussions centered on measured token consumption and business outcome rather than model invocation count
